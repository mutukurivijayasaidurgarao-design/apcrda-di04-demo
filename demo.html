Workflow

Public registers/signs in → selects department → uploads issue photo → describes issue/duration/impact → provides GPS or manual location → submits → Main Office verifies and corrects department if required → department resolves and uploads after-photo → Main Office verifies and closes → citizen receives appreciation message.
